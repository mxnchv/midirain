- **Image:** `promo/thumbnail.png` (o una captura del dispositivo en Live)
- **Video:** el enlace de YouTube

**Description:**

> Rain falls on invisible columns. Each column is a note. Each drop that hits the ground plays it.
>
> midirain is a generative MIDI effect: put it in front of any instrument and let the rain write the melody, from sparse drizzle to dense storms.
>
> - Density, Fall, Wind: how much it rains, how fast and at what angle.
> - Distance: far rain gives thin, quiet drops; close rain gives big, loud ones. It sets the velocity range.
> - Scale mode: the columns are the notes of a scale (11 to choose from) within a range you set.
> - Held mode: the columns are the notes you hold on your MIDI input. Play a chord and it rains on it.
> - Sync: quantize the impacts to the grid, from 1/32 to 1/2, with triplets. Works with Live playing or stopped.
> - Length: the duration of each note.
>
> Every control has a description in Live's Info View. Single frozen .amxd, no extra files.
>
> Try it in the browser: https://mxnchv.xyz/midirain
